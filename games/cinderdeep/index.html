A playable demo

Cinderdeep

Wake at the bottom of a city of wax, find the flicker relic, and put out the Chandler.

In Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Cinderdeep then opens full screen from its own icon and plays offline.

Arrows / WASD move · Space jump · X strike · C flicker · F tap to hurl, hold to mend · M map. Gamepads work too.

Left thumb walks. Right thumb jumps, strikes and casts. Hold your phone sideways.
